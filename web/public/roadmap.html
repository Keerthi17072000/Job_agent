Job Agent Roadmap

This page is the project roadmap. The app itself is on the home page and runs entirely in your browser. A local agent that reads your resume, searches Naukri, Hirist, iimjobs, LinkedIn and company career pages through a Playwright plugin, ranks jobs against your profile, and applies after you approve or automatically within limits you set.

Every portal gets one adapter with the same shape: how to read a listing and, in Phase 2, how to apply. Each portal runs in one of two modes, set by what its terms allow.

Direct mode

  • Career-page feeds (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters) and public job APIs (Remotive, Jobicy, Arbeitnow, RemoteOK, Adzuna with your key)
  • The app fetches listings from your browser when you click Search jobs

Plugin mode

  • For portals that restrict scraping: Naukri and LinkedIn
  • A browser extension runs in your own logged-in tab
  • It reads only the page you have open, and only when you click Send to Job Agent
  • No background crawling, no automated page loads

Phases

BuiltPhase 1

MVP: parse, search, rank, approve

  • Resume upload (PDF or TXT) parsed into skills, years and target roles
  • Preferences: roles, location, minimum score, blocklist
  • Browser extension (plugin mode) sends Naukri and LinkedIn listings from your open tab
  • 0–100 score from skill overlap, title match and experience fit; duplicate jobs merged
  • Approval queue with Approve and open, Skip, Mark applied
NextPhase 2

Apply flow and auto-apply

  • Naukri apply and LinkedIn Easy Apply submission, through the extension so it happens in your own tab
  • Answer bank for screening questions, filled only from your resume and saved answers
  • Auto-apply mode with guardrails (see below)
  • Screenshot and submitted answers saved for every application
PlannedPhase 3

More portals and scheduling

  • Hirist and iimjobs adapters, in plugin mode unless their terms allow direct reading
  • Scheduled daily runs, email or WhatsApp alert when new matches arrive
  • Plugin health check that flags a portal whose layout changed
PlannedPhase 4

Company career pages

  • You add company URLs; the agent finds openings and the apply link
  • Templates for common applicant systems: Greenhouse, Lever, Workday
  • Unknown pages open for you to finish by hand
PlannedPhase 5

Tailoring and analytics

  • Per-job resume tweaks and cover note from the job description
  • Follow-up reminders
  • Which roles, portals and score bands actually get replies

Approval and auto-apply

Ask mode (default)

  • Agent shows the job, score, reasons and the answers it would send
  • You approve, edit or skip each one
  • Nothing is submitted without a tap

Auto mode (optional, off by default)

  • Minimum match score you choose
  • Daily cap, for example 20 applications
  • Company and keyword blocklist
  • Unknown screening question: stop and ask, never guess
  • One-click kill switch

Accuracy Invented answersThe agent states skills or salary you do not have.Answers come only from the resume and your saved answers; anything else is sent to you.

Privacy Personal dataResume details leave your machine.Everything is stored locally; only the text needed for parsing goes to the Claude API.

Open decisions

  • Personal tool or product for many users. A product needs accounts and a legal review of each portal's terms.
  • Which portal gets apply support first. Recommended: Naukri, then LinkedIn Easy Apply.
  • Auto-apply daily cap and minimum score defaults.
